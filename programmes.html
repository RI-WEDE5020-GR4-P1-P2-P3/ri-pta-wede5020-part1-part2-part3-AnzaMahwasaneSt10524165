Our Community Programmes

Explore the core initiatives we run weekly to uplift the youth in Limpopo.

Our Three Core Programmes

Every week our volunteers run a daily nutrition programme, after-school tutoring and youth empowerment workshops. Press play to watch each one, or send an enquiry to get involved.

1. Daily Nutritional Programme

Operating Monday to Friday, we serve balanced hot meals to over 150 local children after school hours, ensuring hunger never hinders their ability to learn.

2. After School Programme

Volunteer tutors assist learners from Grades 4 to 12 with homework, examination preparation, and literacy in English and Mathematics.

3. Youth Empowerment

Held on weekends, these interactive bootcamps cover practical topics such as basic computer usage, career guidance, and leadership skills.
