About Rudzani Community Outreach

A community-driven NPO in Limpopo working for hungry, under-supported and under-mentored youth.

Our Story

Rudzani Community Outreach was founded in 2018 in Tshisaulu, Thohoyandou, Limpopo. The organisation was established to address educational inequality, food insecurity, and a lack of youth mentorship in low-income households.

Today we run a daily nutrition programme, after-school tutoring and youth workshops. If you would like to be part of the story, contact our team or send an enquiry.

Our Mission

To empower youth within the community through quality education support, practical life skills, and daily nutritional care.

Our Vision

A community where every child, regardless of socioeconomic background, has equal opportunities to succeed academically and personally.

Our Goal

Our goal is to uplift and empower young people in Tshisaulu and Thohoyandou by providing daily nutrition and quality after-school tutoring.
